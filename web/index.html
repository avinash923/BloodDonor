Donors are ranked by road travel time and by their predicted likelihood to donate, and the doctor sees each donor who accepts — with name, contact number and last donation.

How matching works

Who is alertedEvery available, eligible donor with a matching blood group (or any compatible group, if the doctor allows) who can reach the hospital by road within the time limit
RankingPredicted donation likelihood × (1 − road travel time ÷ time remaining)
Late joinersDonors who register or share a location later are alerted automatically while the request is open
DeferralDonors are not alerted until their safe donation interval has passed
